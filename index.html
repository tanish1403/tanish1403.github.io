Profile

Computer vision and AI engineer — detection models, 3D medical segmentation, multimodal pipelines.

Computer vision and AI engineer building end-to-end deep learning systems and research-driven vision models — for images that don't cooperate.

M.Tech in Signal and Image Processing at NIT Rourkela (Aug 2024 – Jun 2026, GPA 9.7/10), after a B.Tech in Electronics and Communications Engineering at Assam University, Silchar (8.2/10).

At Samsung R&D Institute India, Bangalore (Jan–Jul 2026) I worked in the Platform Intelligence team on an AI data-generation system: an MLLM-powered quality-check pipeline that scored synthetic training samples by fusing OCR text, image features, vision-language and zero-shot CLIP embeddings into a custom out-of-distribution score.

As a PG researcher in the PRISM group under Dr. Sobhan Kanti Dhara, I designed GeoStatNet (IEEE SPACE 2026, accepted) and GFCR-Net (IEEE GRSL, early access) for small, oriented ship detection, and co-authored SAPNet, TSRNet and CSPNet.

Before that: gigapixel pathology pipelines on Upwork, a YOLOv7 PCB defect detector at MNIT Jaipur, and a TensorFlow recommender at Navodita Infotech.
